<div style="text-align: justify; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 2em;">
This completed GRF project characterized cadmium and mercury halide clusters solvated by water and related ligands under conditions relevant to volcanic and hydrothermal vapors. ESI–FT-ICR mass spectrometry and IRMPD spectroscopy were used to probe composition and hydrogen-bonding environments, while quantum chemical calculations provided vibrational frequencies and solvation-shell structures. The work connects molecular-scale clustering to metal transport in low-density magmatic–hydrothermal fluids.
</div>
