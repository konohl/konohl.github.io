<div style="text-align: justify; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 2em;">
The infrared OPO/OPA laboratory is built around a custom-modified 7&nbsp;T Bruker FT-ICR mass spectrometer with electrospray ionization and a Nd:YAG-pumped optical parametric oscillator / amplifier (OPO/OPA) that delivers tunable mid-infrared radiation for infrared multiphoton dissociation (IRMPD). The beamline supports structure-sensitive spectroscopy of gas-phase metal complexes, solvated ion clusters, and related molecular species under ultrahigh vacuum after mass selection.
</div>

    ---
    Facility:                IRMPD · FT-ICR mass spectrometry
    IR source:               Nd:YAG-pumped OPO/OPA (mid-IR; AgGaSe<sub>2</sub> extension available)
    Mass spectrometer:      7 T Bruker ESI/FT-ICR
    Applications:            Metal–ligand complexes · hydrated clusters · ore-vapor speciation
    Related labs:            UV–vis spectroscopy lab · Fibre-optics cavity ring-down lab

<div style="text-align: justify; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 2em;">
  <b>Science focus.</b> Typical experiments electrospray dilute aqueous metal–ligand solutions, isolate ions in the ICR cell, and record IRMPD action spectra while scanning the OPO/OPA. Spectra are interpreted with DFT and higher-level ab initio frequency calculations. This laboratory underpins the funded GRF programmes on metal transport, ore nanodroplets, and related cluster studies.
</div>
