<div style="text-align: justify; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 2em;">
The fibre-optics cavity ring-down laboratory uses broadband fibre-loop optical cavities (fibre-loop ring-down spectroscopy, FLRDS) operating across roughly 350–1100&nbsp;nm. By circulating light in a fibre cavity rather than a conventional mirror cavity, the setup improves detection limits for weak absorbers and is adapted for coupling to high-pressure / high-temperature aqueous cells used elsewhere in the group.
</div>

    ---
    Facility:                Fibre-loop cavity ring-down spectroscopy (FLRDS)
    Spectral window:         ~350–1100 nm
    Coupling:                High-PT cells · fibre-optic probes
    Applications:            Trace aqueous absorbers · biomolecules under extreme PT · hydrothermal fluids
    Related labs:            UV–vis spectroscopy lab · Infrared OPO/OPA lab
    ---

<div style="text-align: justify; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 2em;">
  <b>Science focus.</b> FLRDS complements single-pass UV–vis transmission through sapphire-windowed hydrothermal cells when higher sensitivity is required. The same Au/Ni-lined cell family and fibre-optic infrastructure serve both approaches, so experiments can move between broadband transmission and cavity-enhanced detection as concentration and path-length demand.
</div>
