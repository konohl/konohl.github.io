<div style="text-align: justify; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 2em;">
This GRF project investigates water nanodroplets and metal-enriched nanodroplets relevant to ore vapors, with emphasis on stability, high-temperature free energies, and infrared spectral signatures of hydrated Au, Ag, and Cu species. Car–Parrinello molecular dynamics is combined with FT-ICR mass spectrometry and IRMPD spectroscopy, and with wavefunction-based structure and frequency calculations for smaller clusters.
</div>
