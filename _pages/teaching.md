### EASC2401 — Fluid/Solid Interactions

Undergraduate course on the physical and chemical principles that govern Earth processes at fluid–solid interfaces. Topics include equilibrium thermodynamics, phase diagrams (melting, crystallization, condensation), energy transfer, reaction kinetics and isotope fractionation, and the forces that drive fluid flow and particle transport. It is a core requirement for Earth System Science and Geology majors.

### EASC6010 — Nanogeoscience

Postgraduate elective on nanomaterial properties and nano-scale processes in solids, fluids and droplets, vapors, and reacting interfaces, with emphasis on Earth and environmental systems. The course covers size- and shape-dependent thermodynamics and phase behavior, experimental and spectroscopic methods for nano-materials, and applications from chemical weathering and atmospheric nanoparticles to early-Earth chemistry and the origins of life.

### CCGL9033 — Science of WMD Systems (Weapons of Mass Destruction)

Common Core course on nuclear, chemical, and biological weapons: their scientific basis, historical development, deterrent and environmental legacy, and the risks posed by proliferation networks and non-state actors. Students examine strategic, political, and ethical dimensions of WMD programmes, non-proliferation treaties and monitoring technology, and the question of whether complete disarmament is feasible or desirable.

### CCST9018 — The Origins and Evolution of Life

Common Core course in astrobiology on how life emerged on early Earth and how it might arise elsewhere. Topics include the roles of liquid water, atmospheric gases, and mineral interfaces in prebiotic chemistry; habitable zones and exoplanets; alternative biochemistries; and the scientific and societal implications of searching for life beyond Earth, including interplanetary and interstellar exploration.
