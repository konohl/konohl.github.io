<p style="text-align: justify;"> <strong>What we do:</strong> we study aqueous fluids using FT mass spectrometry, IR and UV–vis spectroscopy to probe the structure and stability of molecular species in liquids and vapors. <strong>What we operate:</strong> our experimental setup features a modified ICR FT-MS, an OPO/OPA laser with tunable IR photodissociation, and high-PT spectroscopic uv–vis cells with fiber optic coupling and Au-plated interiors. <strong>Why we do this:</strong> this approach allows us to examine the behavior and reactivity of inorganic and organometallic complexes under simulated hydrothermal and prebiotic conditions.</p>

At HKU, I teach courses in Fluid/Solid Interactions `(EASC2401)`, Nanogeoscience`(EASC6010)`, the science of WMD Systems`(CCGL9033)`, and The Origins and Evolution of Life`(CCST9018)`.
