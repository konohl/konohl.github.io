<p style="text-align: justify;"> We study aqueous fluids using FTMS spectrometry and IR spectroscopy to measure the structure and stability of clusters in water and vapor. Our setup includes a modified ICR FT-MS OPO/OPA laser system with tunable IR radiation, and a custom high PT cell (with fiber optics, sapphire windows and Au electroplated interior to prevent surface catalysis) for studying organometallic complex stability - especially prebiotic chemistry - linked to a UV vis spectrophotometer. We also use static and ab initio MD calculations to analyze cluster structures and properties. </p>

As an instructor, I teach courses in fluid geochemistry `(EASC2401)`, the science underpinning WMD system and the implications on proliferation and terrorism`(CCGL9033)`, and the origins of life `(CCST9018)`.
