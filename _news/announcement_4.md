<b>:loudspeaker: New high PT spectrophotometric cell for origins of life studies, Sep.21, 2025 </b> :alembic: :fire: :microscope: :dna: 

<div style="text-align: justify;">
<p>
We are happy to announce the successful installation of a set of new custom-built high PT spectrophotometric cells in our laboratory, developed in collaboration with SITEC AG in Zurich (CH). These gold-lined spectrophotometric cells will enhance our ability to study a wide range biomolecules, such as peptides and nucleosites, at hydrothermal conditions, focusing on the kinetic and thermodynamic stability of these materials at conditions commonly found around seafloor black smokers. These spectrophotometric cells feature a pressure capacity of up to ~2 kbar, temperatures up to 400°C, various sapphire and quartz optical view-ports and a temperature-resistant fiber-optic equipped spectrophotometer for real-time analysis. 
</p>
<p>
This new capability will significantly advance our experimental investigations into the stability and complexation of biomolecule fragments and transition metals, providing insights into the geochemistry that may have influenced the selection of life's first building blocks on the early Earth.
</p>
