<b>:loudspeaker: Wallace successfully defends PhD thesis!, March 14, 2025 </b> 💫🥂🥇 

<div style="text-lign: justify;">
<p>Congratulations to Wallace on successfully defending his PhD thesis on theoretical studies of clustering, ionization, and complexation in hydrothermal fluids and nanodroplets! This outstanding work culminates 11 years of fruitful collaboration, blending exceptional published papers with fresh insights into the unique structures and properties of water in these extreme environments. Well done, Wallace! Your work has advanced our understanding of metal and volatile transport in volcanic and hydrothermal systems, in particular, it has shown how nanodroplets control metal and volatile speciation in these environments.


</p>
