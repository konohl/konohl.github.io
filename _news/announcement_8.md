<b>Special Issue of Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta: Hydrothermal Geochemistry and Beyond – A Tribute to Terry M. Seward

<div style="text-align: justify;">
<p>
The Special Issue "<a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/geochimica-et-cosmochimica-acta/vol/406/suppl/C">Hydrothermal Geochemistry and Beyond: A Tribute to Terry M. Seward</a>" has been published in <b>Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta</b> (406, 5-339) and is now available online. This collection of papers honors Terry’s foundational contributions to hydrothermal geochemistry, featuring studies on metal complexation, solubility, isotope fractionation, and fluid-mineral interactions by his colleagues, students, and collaborators. We (Jean-Francois, Andri and myself) would like to extend our gratitude to the authors, reviewers, and the Elsevier editorial team—specially Jeff Catalano—for their efforts in realizing this tribute.
</p>
