<b>:loudspeaker: New FO-CRDS spectrometer for prebiotic studies, Nov.19, 2026 </b> :bulb: :gem: :droplet: 

<div style="text-align: justify;">
<p>
We are happy to announce the successful commissioning of a new fibre-optic cavity ring-down spectrometer (FO-CRDS) in our laboratory. A 460 nm laser diode has been successfully pumped into a fibre-optic cavity roughly 1.8 m long. Cavity ring-down measures absorption from the decay time of light trapped in an optical cavity, here a fibre-optic cavity, giving very high sensitivity to weak optical absorptions and to small sample volumes. The instrument uses a pulsed diode laser source, fibre cavity, fast photodetection and oscilloscope recordings of the ring-down traces, and complements our existing high-PT UV–vis cells and OPO/OPA IR laser work. 
</p>
<p>
This new capability will significantly advance our experimental investigations of aqueous and hydrothermal fluids and biomolecule and metal-complex chemistry. Once coupled to a diamond anvil cell (DAC), FO-CRDS will be used to identify and monitor prebiotic systems at extreme pressures.
</p>
