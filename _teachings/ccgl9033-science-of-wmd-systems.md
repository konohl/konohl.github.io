## Course Overview

Common Core course on nuclear, chemical, and biological weapons: their scientific basis, historical development, deterrent and environmental legacy, and the risks posed by proliferation networks and non-state actors. Students examine strategic, political, and ethical dimensions of WMD programmes, non-proliferation treaties and monitoring technology, and the question of whether complete disarmament is feasible or desirable.
