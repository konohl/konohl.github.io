## Course Overview

Common Core course in astrobiology on how life emerged on early Earth and how it might arise elsewhere. Topics include the roles of liquid water, atmospheric gases, and mineral interfaces in prebiotic chemistry; habitable zones and exoplanets; alternative biochemistries; and the scientific and societal implications of searching for life beyond Earth, including interplanetary and interstellar exploration.
