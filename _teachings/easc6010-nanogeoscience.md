## Course Overview

This course provides an overview of nanomaterial properties and nano-scale processes that take place in solids, fluids/droplets, vapors and across reacting interfaces, with an emphasis on the Earth and environmental sciences.

Students will:

- Understand basic concepts of nanoscience, nanotechnology and Earth materials
- Understand how shape and size influence electronic structure and properties of nanomaterials
- Apply concepts of nanoscience to Earth and environmental science systems

## Prerequisites

- None
