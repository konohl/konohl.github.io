## Course Overview

Postgraduate elective on nanomaterial properties and nano-scale processes in solids, fluids and droplets, vapors, and reacting interfaces, with emphasis on Earth and environmental systems. The course covers size- and shape-dependent thermodynamics and phase behavior, experimental and spectroscopic methods for nano-materials, and applications from chemical weathering and atmospheric nanoparticles to early-Earth chemistry and the origins of life.
