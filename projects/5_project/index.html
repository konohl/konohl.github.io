UV–vis spectroscopy lab

Laboratory 1 | High-PT UV–vis cells with sapphire windows and fibre optics for aqueous speciation near 400 °C and 1 kbar.

The UV–visible spectroscopy laboratory supports in-situ studies of aqueous organic and inorganic speciation under hydrothermal and prebiotic conditions. Central to the facility are custom high-pressure / high-temperature spectroscopic cells with Au- or Ni-lined bodies, single-crystal sapphire viewports, and high-temperature fibre-optic coupling for transmission measurements. The cells are designed for operation up to about 400–450 °C and pressures on the order of 1 kbar (~100 MPa; selected cells toward 200 MPa), enabling continuous optical monitoring of reacting fluids without quenching.

Science focus. Typical experiments follow the stability and reactivity of dissolved species (including biomolecules and metal complexes) as temperature and pressure are ramped, using broadband UV–vis absorption through the sapphire windows. Together with the infrared OPO/OPA and fibre-optics cavity ring-down laboratories, this facility forms the group’s experimental spectroscopy suite.
