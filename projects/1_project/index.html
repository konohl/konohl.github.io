Metal transport in ore fluids

GRF 17308423 (2023–2027) | FT-MS and IRMPD of Au- and Cu–organic complexes in aqueous ore fluids and related biomolecule clusters.

This GRF project examines Au(III) and Cu(I) chloride complexes with organic and organosulfur ligands using high-resolution FT-ICR mass spectrometry and IRMPD spectroscopy, with the aim of clarifying how such ligands influence gold and copper transport in aqueous ore fluids. A second theme focuses on the same metals bound to amino acids and dipeptides, addressing metal–biomolecule interactions, oligomerization, and possible roles in prebiotic chemistry. Experiment is paired with DFT and higher-level ab initio calculations of structures, spectra, and complexation free energies.

Methods. Dilute aqueous metal–ligand solutions are electrosprayed into the FT-ICR; ions are mass-selected and irradiated with tunable IR light to record IRMPD spectra. Complementary structure searches (including molecular dynamics / PSO where useful) are followed by DFT reoptimization and frequency analysis, with selected low-energy isomers refined at MP2 or CCSD(T) with larger basis sets. Production calculations run on HKU HPC facilities (Gaussian, CFOUR, CP2K).
