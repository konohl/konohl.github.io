Nanogeoscience

Postgraduate elective on nanomaterial properties and nano-scale processes in solids, fluids and droplets, vapors, and reacting interfaces, with emphasis on Earth and environmental systems. The course covers size- and shape-dependent thermodynamics and phase behavior, experimental and spectroscopic methods for nano-materials, and applications from chemical weathering and atmospheric nanoparticles to early-Earth chemistry and the origins of life.

Number: EASC6010

Instructor: Kono H. Lemke

Term: Second semester

Course Overview

This course provides an overview of nanomaterial properties and nano-scale processes that take place in solids, fluids/droplets, vapors and across reacting interfaces, with an emphasis on the Earth and environmental sciences.

Students will:

  • Understand basic concepts of nanoscience, nanotechnology and Earth materials
  • Understand how shape and size influence electronic structure and properties of nanomaterials
  • Apply concepts of nanoscience to Earth and environmental science systems

Prerequisites

  • None

Grading

  • Project work: 40%
  • Assignments 1–6: 10% each

Each student takes their own research and develops its nanoscale aspects and features.
